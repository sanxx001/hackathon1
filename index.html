The Challenge

Restaurants, caterers, and grocers frequently discard quality edible food because they lack immediate channels to notify local soup kitchens and shelters before the food passes its safe consumption window.

🌱

Our Solution

A decentralized, zero-barrier platform enabling donors to publish surplus batches in seconds, pairing them with nearby verified charities using transparent, distance-and-urgency matching rules.

Smart Donation Matcher

Simulated matching engine that ranks available surplus food for your charity using transparent distance, quantity, and deadline urgency heuristics.

Receiving Charity Criteria

Input your organization's location and current meal capacity to find optimal matches.

How Smart Ranking Works:
  • Proximity (40 pts): Prioritizes exact zone match to cut transit time.
  • Portion Fit (35 pts): Matches batch size against charity meal capacity.
  • Urgency (25 pts): Items near expiry rank highest so they get rescued first!
  • Category Bonus (+10 pts): Boosts requested meal type.
*Deterministic simulation. Does not invoke paid AI APIs.
